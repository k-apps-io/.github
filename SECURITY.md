# Security Policy

## Supported Scope

This repository contains organization governance defaults and automation configuration.
Security concerns include:

- Leaked credentials or secrets in workflows or templates.
- Permission misconfiguration in automation.
- Supply-chain risk introduced by third-party actions.

## Reporting a Vulnerability

Do not open a public issue for sensitive disclosures.

1. Email: `security@k-apps.io`
2. Include:
   - Summary and impact
   - Reproduction steps
   - Suggested remediation (if known)
3. Provide a contact method for follow-up.

## Response Expectations

- Initial triage target: within 2 business days.
- Status updates provided during investigation.
- Remediation and disclosure timing coordinated with maintainers.

## Handling Guidelines

- Rotate compromised credentials immediately.
- Restrict affected automation until mitigated.
- Record incident decisions and follow-up hardening actions.
